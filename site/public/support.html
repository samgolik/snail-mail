a small help desk for a slow post office

support

having trouble with an account, friend code, or letter? write to beattheclockstudios@gmail.com. include what you expected, what happened, your iPhone model, and iOS version. please never email your password.

account deletion

open you, scroll to account, and choose delete account. if you cannot access the app, email us from the address on the account.

letters arrive slowly by design

arrival is calculated from the cities on both profiles and enforced by server time. changing the phone’s clock will not deliver a letter early.
