a small help desk for a slow post office

support

having trouble with an account, friend code, or letter? write to beattheclockstudios@gmail.com. include what you expected, what happened, your iPhone model, and iOS version. never send us an SMS verification code.

account deletion

open you, scroll to account, and choose delete account. if you cannot access the app, email support for help identifying the account safely.

letters arrive slowly by design

arrival is calculated from the cities on both profiles and enforced by server time. changing the phone’s clock will not deliver a letter early.

safety

report or block an account from a received letter’s safety menu or under you → friends. read the community guidelines for what is not allowed and how reports are handled.
