privacy, slowly and plainly

snail mail is made by beat the clock studios. we collect only the information needed to create accounts, connect friends, and deliver delayed messages.

information we process

  • your verified mobile phone number and Firebase account identifier;
  • the display name, username, city, approximate city coordinates, and friend code you add to your profile;
  • friend requests, connections, blocks, safety reports, and the version and time of your terms acceptance;
  • letter contents and delivery metadata, including sender, recipient, cities, and timestamps; and
  • basic technical information and Apple App Attest security proofs processed by Firebase to authenticate requests and keep the service secure.

how it is used

we use this information only to provide and protect app functionality, including reviewing safety reports and enforcing blocks. we do not sell personal information, use third-party advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

sealed letters

letter bodies are stored separately from mailbox metadata. the app cannot read a body directly. our server releases it only to the authenticated recipient after the server-calculated arrival time.

profile privacy

friends and past correspondents receive only your display name, username, and city. your private friend code and the approximate coordinates used to calculate delivery time are returned only to your own signed-in account.

sharing and service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication, database, server, and hosting infrastructure. phone numbers are sent to and stored by Google for authentication, spam prevention, and abuse prevention. information may be processed by Google as our service provider under its applicable data-processing terms. we may disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

retention and deletion

information remains while your account is active. you can permanently delete your account inside the app under you → account → delete account. this removes your profile, connections, letters, sealed bodies, reservations, reports you submitted, and authentication account. a limited record of reports about an account may be retained when needed to prevent abuse or meet legal obligations. backups and security logs may persist for a limited period before routine deletion.

children

snail mail is not directed to children under 13. if you believe a child provided information, contact us so we can remove it.
