community guidelines

snail mail should feel like receiving a thoughtful letter, not opening a door to harm. treat every person behind an address with care.

not allowed

  • harassment, bullying, threats, stalking, or encouragement of violence or self-harm;
  • hateful or dehumanizing content targeting protected characteristics;
  • sexual exploitation, sexual content involving minors, or non-consensual intimate content;
  • impersonation, fraud, scams, spam, or repeated unwanted contact;
  • sharing another person’s sensitive personal information without permission;
  • illegal content or using the service to plan or facilitate illegal acts; or
  • evading a block, suspension, rate limit, or other safety control.

report, block, and step away

use a received letter’s safety menu to report the letter or block its sender. friend controls under you also let you report, block, or remove a connection. reports are private. blocking immediately removes the connection and prevents new letters or requests in either direction.

what happens after a report

beat the clock studios may review the reported account, letter, and relevant service records. outcomes may include no action, a warning, feature restrictions, suspension, termination, preservation of evidence, or referral when required for safety or law. we aim to review urgent safety reports within 24 hours during an active beta.

appeals and urgent danger

to appeal an account action, email beattheclockstudios@gmail.com and include your profile username. never send an SMS verification code. if someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services; an in-app report is not an emergency service.
