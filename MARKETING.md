# Snail Mail launch marketing plan

Updated November 11, 2026, following the public App Store launch of version 1.1.

## Positioning

**Category:** slow social messaging  
**Promise:** messages worth waiting for  
**One-line pitch:** Send a private digital letter and let a tiny fictional snail
carry it to your friend.

Lead with the behavior, not the feature list: **send now, open later**. The
strongest contrast is with noisy, instant, disposable messaging—not physical
postal mail.

## App Store discovery

The app launched today and is retrievable by Apple ID, but currently has no
ratings. A new listing may not immediately rank for a broad, competitive query.
Apple says search considers text relevance plus behavior such as downloads,
ratings, and reviews.

Current indexed metadata already contains the exact phrase **snail mail** in the
app name. For version 1.2, replace duplicated keyword terms with:

`letters,friends,delayed,penpal,private,thoughtful,connection,postcard,correspondence,asynchronous`

Do not repeat words already in the app name, subtitle, or category. Promotional
text improves conversion but is not indexed for search.

Immediate actions:

1. Send traffic through the direct App Store URL while organic indexing settles.
2. Ask real, engaged users for an honest rating after a successful letter open;
   never gate, script, or incentivize reviews.
3. Review App Store tags under App Information and keep all relevant suggested
   tags selected.
4. Monitor App Analytics weekly: impressions, product-page views, conversion,
   downloads, and source type = App Store Search.
5. Test the three search creatives in `Marketing/AppStoreSearch/` using Product
   Page Optimization, changing one creative hypothesis at a time.
6. Start Apple Ads only after the organic product page converts reasonably.
   Begin with a small daily cap and exact-match intent terms—not broad match.

## TikTok content system

Use screen recordings from a real device, captions burned into the edit, and a
direct App Store link in the profile. Keep most posts between 8 and 18 seconds.
The first frame must communicate the entire premise without sound.

### Repeatable formats

1. **The premise** — “I made an app where your texts travel at a snail’s pace.”
   Show Send, the journey screen, then the sealed letter.
2. **Distance test** — “How long would a snail take from DC to California?” Show
   two recipients at different distances and their different delivery times.
3. **Anticipation** — “POV: your friend sent something yesterday and the app
   still won’t let you open it.” Show the seal and arrival countdown.
4. **Founder build story** — old Intel Mac, command-line builds, Firebase issues,
   TestFlight, then the live App Store page.
5. **No-pressure social** — “No feed. No likes. No read receipts. Just one letter
   worth waiting for.” Show the snailbox and Snail Trail.
6. **Friend reaction** — film a consenting friend opening a letter that finally
   arrived. Never expose private letter text without explicit permission.
7. **Feature request loop** — ask viewers where the snail should travel next or
   which stamp/design they want; respond to comments with videos.
8. **Build in public** — one bug, one decision, or one tiny visual polish per post.

### First seven posts

| Day | Hook | Visual | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I made texting unnecessarily slow | send → journey → arrival | search “snail mail: slow messages” |
| 2 | DC to California, but at snail speed | route comparison | where should I send one next? |
| 3 | you literally cannot open it early | sealed letter close-up | would this make messages better? |
| 4 | building an iPhone app without modern Xcode | terminal → phone → App Store | follow the build story |
| 5 | no likes, feeds, or follower counts | calm UI montage | send this to your pen pal |
| 6 | my friend finally opened the letter | reaction + arrival notification | download link in bio |
| 7 | the features TikTok asked for | comment screenshots + roadmap | comment the next feature |

Measure profile visits, App Store product-page views, and downloads—not views
alone. Repeat hooks that drive profile visits even if their raw view count is
smaller.

## Paid campaign guardrails

- Start at **$5–10/day maximum** with a hard campaign cap.
- Run only in the United States initially.
- Use exact-match terms such as `slow messaging`, `digital letters`, `pen pal`,
  and the branded term `snail mail slow messages`.
- Add unrelated physical-mail, shipping, USPS, and package-tracking searches as
  negative keywords.
- Pause any term after meaningful spend without installs; do not let automated
  broad targeting expand unchecked.
